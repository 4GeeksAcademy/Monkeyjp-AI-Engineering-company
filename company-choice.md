# Company Choice

## Selected Company: Brasaland

I chose Brasaland because I find it interesting to work with a company that combines traditional restaurant operations with the need for a complete digital transformation. The company has 14 restaurants located across Colombia and the United States, which means it must manage operations in two countries, two currencies, and potentially two languages.

I am also interested in the fact that many of its current processes still depend on phone calls, WhatsApp messages, paper documents, and spreadsheets. This makes it possible to identify specific problems that could be solved through web applications, automation, data analysis, and artificial intelligence.

Another aspect that interests me is that the solutions developed would have a clear and easy-to-understand impact. For example, they could reduce food waste, improve ingredient availability, simplify employee training, and provide customers with a more personalized experience.

In addition, Brasaland needs to build much of its technological infrastructure almost from scratch, including a central API, telemetry systems, and dashboards. This creates opportunities to work on both customer-facing products and internal tools for employees and managers.

## Departments I Find Most Interesting

### Restaurant Operations

I find this department especially interesting because each restaurant currently operates in a largely isolated way. Managers do not have a centralized, real-time view of sales, inventory, or the performance of each location.

I would like to work on a solution that makes it possible to monitor sales by restaurant, detect problems during opening hours, and improve ingredient ordering by using historical information and current inventory data.

### Training and Quality Standards

I am also interested in this department because Brasaland needs to maintain the same recipes, preparation techniques, and presentation standards across all of its restaurants.

Currently, the training documents are stored in a shared Google Drive that is difficult to navigate, and updates can take several days to reach every location. A training platform with search functionality, organized content, and support for both Spanish and English could significantly improve this process.

### Technology

The Technology department would be essential for enabling the other solutions to work properly. Brasaland currently does not have an internal API, consolidated data, or real-time telemetry.

I would be interested in helping create a central API that connects data related to restaurants, sales, menus, customers, inventory, and suppliers. This infrastructure could later support dashboards, automation workflows, and artificial intelligence solutions.

## Selected Automation Challenge

The challenge I would most like to develop is an intelligent system for predicting ingredient demand for each restaurant.

The system would use historical sales, available inventory, the day of the week, the restaurant's location, and other relevant factors to estimate how much of each ingredient will be needed over the next few days.

The goal would be to prevent both product shortages and excessive ingredient purchases that could lead to unnecessary waste.

## My AI Agent Idea

I would create an AI agent to help restaurant managers control inventory and prepare ingredient orders.

The agent would receive information about recent sales, current stock levels, pending orders, ingredient consumption history, and demand forecasts. It could also consider factors such as the day of the week, the best-selling products at each location, and upcoming promotions.

Based on this information, the agent would generate a proposed order for each supplier. For example, it could indicate that a restaurant needs to order a specific amount of meat, vegetables, beverages, or packaging to meet the expected demand for the next few days.

The restaurant manager would be able to review and approve the proposal before sending it to the supplier. The agent could also generate alerts when it detects a risk of running out of an important ingredient or when current stock levels are significantly higher than necessary.
